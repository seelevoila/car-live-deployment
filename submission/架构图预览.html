04-改稿状态机


stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> Idle
    Idle --> Loading: 提交脚本
    Loading --> Playing: 首包到达
    Playing --> Playing: 继续播放
    Playing --> Paused: 暂停
    Paused --> Playing: 继续
    Playing --> EditRequested: 改稿
    EditRequested --> ComputeBoundary: 计算边界
    ComputeBoundary --> CancelPending: 取消待生成
    CancelPending --> Playing: 新稿
    Playing --> [*]: 完成
